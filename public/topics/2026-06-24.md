### Agent Infrastructure
- **Hermes Agent gets a Stripe wallet** - Nous Research shipped Stripe skills for Hermes Agent, so agents can buy things, pay per-call APIs, and provision SaaS under configurable limits. The real trust boundary is spend approval and controlled limits, not giving an agent an unlimited card.
  Post: https://x.com/NousResearch/status/2066647737613832624

### Models & Research
- **GLM-5.2 ships open weights with a 1M context** - [[Z.ai]]'s GLM-5.2 jumps past GLM-5.1 with solid 1M-token context, stronger coding and agentic task performance, and an MIT license with no regional limits. Two reasoning modes trade latency for performance, and IndexShare sparse attention cuts per-token FLOPs 2.9× at 1M context. Same API pricing as GLM-5.1.
  Source: https://z.ai/blog/glm-5.2
  Source: https://huggingface.co/zai-org/GLM-5.2
  Source: https://github.com/zai-org/GLM-5
  Source: https://docs.z.ai/guides/llm/glm-5.2
  Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.15763
  Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.12201
  Post: https://x.com/Zai_org/status/2066938937344495629

- **DiffusionGemma makes text diffusion practical** - Google's experimental 26B MoE text-diffusion model drafts and refines 256-token blocks in parallel, claiming 1000+ tok/s on H100 and 700+ tok/s on RTX 5090. Apache 2.0 weights are on Hugging Face, but quality still trails standard autoregressive Gemma for production text.
  Source: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/diffusion-gemma-faster-text-generation/
  Source: https://developers.googleblog.com/en/diffusiongemma-the-developer-guide
  Source: https://huggingface.co/google/diffusiongemma-26B-A4B-it
  Post: https://x.com/Google/status/2064741293163418032
  Post: https://x.com/sundarpichai/status/2064744345216196942
