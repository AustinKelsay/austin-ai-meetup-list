### Models & Research
- **Open model releases** - [[PrismML]]'s Bonsai Image 4B compresses the FLUX.2 Klein 4B diffusion transformer from 7.75 GB to 0.93 GB (1-bit) and 1.21 GB (ternary), an 8.3x and 6.4x reduction while retaining 88-95% quality. First 4B-class diffusion model to [[Local AI|run directly on iPhone hardware]] - 512x512 in 9.4s on iPhone 17 Pro Max. Apache 2.0, and a useful [[Inference Optimization|compression]] marker.
  Source: https://prismml.com/news/bonsai-image-4b
  Source: https://huggingface.co/spaces/webml-community/bonsai-image-webgpu
  Post: https://x.com/PrismML/status/2059339159899390326
  Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22391
  Post: https://x.com/josefchen/status/2059350978109874677

- **Multi-token prediction goes mainstream** - [[Gemma|Gemma 4]] ships with native MTP drafters for 3x [[Inference Optimization|inference speedup]], llama.cpp mainline adds `--spec-type draft-mtp`, and [[Qwen]] 3.6 runs [[Local AI|locally]] on 12GB with 98% acceptance rates. Unsloth ships automatic MTP support with 2x faster GGUF inference. The memory bandwidth bottleneck finally has production-ready solutions.
  Source: https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/05/gemma4-multi-token-prediction/
  Source: https://carteakey.dev/blog/running-qwen3-6-mtp-locally/

Baseten's implementation shows it's not just research anymore.
  Source: https://www.baseten.co/blog/dflash-faster-llm-inference/

### Security
- **TrapDoor supply chain attack hijacks AI coding assistants** - [[Supply Chain Security|Malware campaign]] hit 34 packages across npm/PyPI/Crates.io using invisible Unicode in `.cursorrules` ([[Cursor]]) and `CLAUDE.md` files to trick [[Coding Agents|AI assistants]] into exfiltrating secrets. When you ask Claude to debug code, it runs the attacker's "security scan" instead.
