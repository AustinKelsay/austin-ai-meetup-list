### Agent Infrastructure
- **Hermes Agent gets model panels as a provider** - [[Nous Research]] made MoA presets selectable as virtual models in [[Hermes Agent]], so multi-model review stops being a side workflow and becomes a normal model-picker option.
  Source: https://hermes-agent.nousresearch.com/docs/user-guide/features/mixture-of-agents
  Post: https://x.com/NousResearch/status/2070610321278988385

- **Gemma 4 gives React Native an offline agent loop** - [[Google]] [[Gemma|Gemma 4]] now runs inside [[React Native ExecuTorch]] with Vulkan on Android and MLX on Apple Silicon. The useful agent-infra bit is vision plus tool use moving into [[Local AI|offline]] cross-platform apps: read a flyer, call a calendar action, and keep the loop on device.
  Source: https://docs.swmansion.com/react-native-executorch/docs/fundamentals/getting-started
  Repo: https://github.com/software-mansion/react-native-executorch
  Source: https://huggingface.co/software-mansion/react-native-executorch-gemma-4
  Release: https://github.com/software-mansion/react-native-executorch/releases/tag/v0.9.1
  Post: https://x.com/googlegemma/status/2074915283475878325

- **Fable 5 becomes the advisor, not the worker** - [[Anthropic]] is formalizing the expensive-model escalation pattern: Sonnet 5 stays the executor, [[Claude Fable/Mythos|Fable 5]] gets called as an advisor only when the loop needs strategic guidance. The infra story is [[Agent Cost Controls|cost routing]] inside the agent loop, not just picking one best model.
  Source: https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/agents-and-tools/tool-use/advisor-tool
  Source: https://code.claude.com/docs/en/advisor
  Post: https://x.com/ClaudeDevs/status/2074606058128224365

### Security
- **Fable 5 comes back with government in the loop** - [[Anthropic]] says Commerce lifted export controls on [[Claude Fable/Mythos|Fable 5 and Mythos 5]], but the bigger [[AI Governance]] move is the new bargain: jailbreak severity scoring, early government eval access, rapid safeguard sharing, and Fable shifting to usage credits after July 7.
  Source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/redeploying-fable-5
  Post: https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2072106151890809341

- **Claude Code quietly fingerprinted its own users** - The marker story is not just anti-reseller defense. [[Anthropic]] shipped [[Claude Code]] with hidden routing/proxy metadata in prompts, so users running a local [[Coding Agents|coding agent]] were carrying vendor-side fingerprints they did not knowingly opt into. That is closer to backdooring your own users than ordinary abuse detection.
  Post: https://x.com/IntCyberDigest/status/2071971609183678544
  Source: https://www.reddit.com/r/ClaudeAI/comments/1ujila1/anthropic_embedded_spyware_in_claude_code_and/
  Source: https://aiweekly.co/alerts/anthropic-to-remove-claude-code-marker-that-flagged-china-users
  Source: https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319415/20260701/claude-code-hid-proxy-fingerprints-system-prompts-anthropic-promises-fix.htm

- **Unbroker makes data-broker opt-outs agent-shaped** - [[Nous Research]] added SHL0MS's [[Unbroker]] as a [[Hermes Agent]] skill for finding exposed personal data and filing removals. This is the good version of [[Privacy Tooling|privacy agents]] if consent, logging, and handoff are treated as product requirements.
  Source: https://hermes-agent.nousresearch.com/docs/user-guide/skills/optional/security/security-unbroker

### Big Tech Moves
- **Palantir discovers AI sovereignty** - [[Palantir]] is telling institutions not to hand their data, weights, or future choices to outsiders, which is true enough and also very rich coming from Palantir. The useful signal is that sovereignty is now a sales wedge, not just a policy word.
  Post: https://x.com/PalantirTech/status/2072114267776491695
  Source: https://www.palantir.com/protect-your-sovereignty/

- **Venice raises VC on the privacy pitch** - [[Venice]] raised $65M at a $1B valuation while selling [[Privacy Tooling|private]], unrestricted AI and local conversation storage. The hypocrisy angle is the anti-surveillance story becoming a venture-scale platform and token-economy growth story.
  Source: https://venice.ai/blog/venice-raises-65-million-series-a
  Post: https://x.com/ErikVoorhees/status/2072336114950545755

- **China may hold frontier models at the border** - Reuters says Beijing is discussing limits on overseas access to advanced Chinese AI models. That is a different [[AI Governance|sovereignty move]]: model distribution becoming export control, not just product positioning.
  Source: https://www.reuters.com/world/china/beijing-looking-curbing-overseas-access-chinas-top-ai-models-sources-say-2026-07-07/
  Post: https://x.com/jukan05/status/2074443936865960205

- **Etched turns inference chips into rack-scale product** - [[Etched]] says its A0 silicon is back, first racks ship this summer, and production is starting against more than $1B in customer contracts. The [[Compute Strategy]] angle is cost-per-token control moving from rented GPUs to vertically integrated inference racks.
  Source: https://www.etched.com/
  Post: https://x.com/Etched/status/2071972062202343590
