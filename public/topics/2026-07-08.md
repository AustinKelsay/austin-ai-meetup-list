### Models & Research
- **Brain2Qwerty v2 gets non-invasive decoding to 61% word accuracy** - [[Meta]]'s MEG-based brain-to-text work turns the BCI story from surgery-only demo into a scaling question: 22,000 typed sentences, nine participants, 10 hours each, and open code/data around a 61% average word-accuracy result.
  Source: https://ai.meta.com/blog/brain2qwerty-brain-ai-human-communication/
  Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-026-02303-2
  Source: https://facebookresearch.github.io/brain2qwerty/

- **Open model releases get specialized fast** - The last-two-week [[Open Models|open-model]] pass is a specialization story, not a rerun of June 24's GLM, Kimi, MiniMax, or TMax slide. [[Qwen]] models agent environments, Mistral goes after Lean proofs, [[Cohere]] opens Arabic ASR, and [[NVIDIA]] turns Nemotron-3 Super into a Puzzle-compressed 75B/9B-active [[Inference Optimization|serving model]] with BF16, FP8, and NVFP4 checkpoints.

- **Should chat route local by default?** - Stanford's IPW paper says [[Local AI|local LMs]] can answer 88.7% of single-turn chat/reasoning queries, while local coverage rose to 71.3%; Ollama's Gemma 4 MTP speedup makes the same point from the runtime side. The board question is what should route local by default.
  Source: https://scalingintelligence.stanford.edu/pubs/ipw/
  Source: https://ollama.com/blog/faster-gemma-4-mlx-mtp
  Source: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/multi-token-prediction-gemma-4/
  Post: https://x.com/ClementDelangue/status/2071951499660292496

- **Proprietary releases split across agents and media** - [[OpenAI]] previewed GPT-5.6 Sol/Terra/Luna through a controlled partner rollout, [[Anthropic]] shipped Claude Sonnet 5 as a cheaper agentic default, and [[Google]] priced Nano Banana 2 Lite plus Gemini Omni Flash for media loops. Closed labs are pulling two directions: frontier reasoning gets more gated while gen-media gets cheap enough to become tooling.
  Source: https://openai.com/index/previewing-gpt-5-6-sol/
  Source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-sonnet-5

- **Claude gets a consciousness-adjacent workspace** - [[Anthropic]]'s J-space paper is not just interpretability tooling; it is a consciousness-adjacent claim. They argue Claude has a small global workspace it can report, steer, and reason through, while explicitly stopping short of saying it feels anything.
  Source: https://www.anthropic.com/research/global-workspace

- **MXFP4 makes MiniMax M3 runnable** - [[MiniMax]] M3's 428B-parameter, 1M-context spec is the backdrop. The sharper story is efficiency: MXFP4 compresses the routed MoE experts, cuts the checkpoint to roughly 237 GB, and the vLLM/MSA/EAGLE3 recipes turn a model-card flex into something people can actually serve.

### Security
- **Fable 5 comes back with government in the loop** - [[Anthropic]] says Commerce lifted export controls on Fable 5 and Mythos 5, but the bigger [[AI Governance]] move is the new bargain: jailbreak severity scoring, early government eval access, rapid safeguard sharing, and Fable shifting to usage credits after July 7.
  Source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/redeploying-fable-5
  Post: https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2072106151890809341
  Post: https://x.com/synthwavedd/status/2072103052635451559
  Post: https://x.com/deredleritt3r/status/2072112364690833604
  Post: https://x.com/theo/status/2072173365318840573

- **Claude Code quietly fingerprinted its own users** - The marker story is not just anti-reseller defense. [[Anthropic]] shipped Claude Code with hidden routing/proxy metadata in prompts, so users running a local [[Coding Agents|coding agent]] were carrying vendor-side fingerprints they did not knowingly opt into. That is closer to backdooring your own users than ordinary abuse detection.
  Post: https://x.com/IntCyberDigest/status/2071971609183678544
  Source: https://www.reddit.com/r/ClaudeAI/comments/1ujila1/anthropic_embedded_spyware_in_claude_code_and/
  Source: https://aiweekly.co/alerts/anthropic-to-remove-claude-code-marker-that-flagged-china-users
  Source: https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319415/20260701/claude-code-hid-proxy-fingerprints-system-prompts-anthropic-promises-fix.htm

- **Unbroker makes data-broker opt-outs agent-shaped** - [[Nous Research]] added SHL0MS's Unbroker as a Hermes Agent skill for finding exposed personal data and filing removals. This is the good version of [[Privacy Tooling|privacy agents]] if consent, logging, and handoff are treated as product requirements.
  Source: https://hermes-agent.nousresearch.com/docs/user-guide/skills/optional/security/security-unbroker
  Source: https://hermes-agent.nousresearch.com/docs/reference/optional-skills-catalog
  Post: https://x.com/SHL0MS/status/2073128911429668877

### Big Tech Moves
- **Palantir discovers AI sovereignty** - Palantir is telling institutions not to hand their data, weights, or future choices to outsiders, which is true enough and also very rich coming from Palantir. The useful signal is that sovereignty is now a sales wedge, not just a policy word.
  Post: https://x.com/PalantirTech/status/2072114267776491695
  Source: https://www.palantir.com/protect-your-sovereignty/

- **Venice raises VC on the privacy pitch** - Venice raised $65M at a $1B valuation while selling private, unrestricted AI and local conversation storage. The hypocrisy angle is the anti-surveillance story becoming a venture-scale platform and token-economy growth story.
  Source: https://venice.ai/blog/venice-raises-65-million-series-a
  Post: https://x.com/ErikVoorhees/status/2072336114950545755

- **China may hold frontier models at the border** - Reuters says Beijing is discussing limits on overseas access to advanced Chinese AI models. That is a different sovereignty move: model distribution becoming export control, not just product positioning.
  Source: https://www.reuters.com/world/china/beijing-looking-curbing-overseas-access-chinas-top-ai-models-sources-say-2026-07-07/
  Post: https://x.com/jukan05/status/2074443936865960205

- **Etched turns inference chips into rack-scale product** - Etched says its A0 silicon is back, first racks ship this summer, and production is starting against more than $1B in customer contracts. The [[Compute Strategy]] angle is cost-per-token control moving from rented GPUs to vertically integrated inference racks.
  Source: https://www.etched.com/
  Post: https://x.com/Etched/status/2071972062202343590
