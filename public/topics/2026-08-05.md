### Models & Research
- **FLUX 3 collapses image, video, audio, and action into one backbone** - [[FLUX 3|Black Forest Labs]] shipped early access for a unified multimodal flow model that jointly learns image, video, and audio, then extends the same backbone to action prediction. Video with native audio is live now; image early access and open-weight [[FLUX 3|FLUX 3 Dev]] come later.
  Source: https://bfl.ai/blog/flux-3
  Post: https://x.com/bfl_ai/status/2080308988961554582

- **Ling-3.0-flash bets 5.1B active can replace a 1T flagship** - Ant Group's [[Ling-3.0-flash]] is a 124B hybrid-reasoning MoE with 5.1B active per token and a native 256K context extendable to 1M. The pitch is production agents that match or beat their 1T flagship on most shown benchmarks.
  Source: https://developer.ant-ling.com/en/docs/models/ling
  Source: https://huggingface.co/inclusionAI/Ling-3.0-flash
  Post: https://x.com/AntLingAGI/status/2080351022028095681

- **Local open drops keep specializing by size and job** - Fresh [[Open Models|open]] packaging centers on Poolside's [[Laguna S 2.1]] 118B/8B-active [[Coding Agents|coding]] MoE with 1M context, Nanbeige 4.2 3B, Solar-Open2 250B, and OpenZero Gemma4-E4B builds. Treat the Bonsai and BTL-3 repeats as packaging noise; the room question is which fit the hardware people own.
  Source: https://poolside.ai/blog/introducing-laguna-s-2-1
  Source: https://huggingface.co/poolside/Laguna-S-2.1
  Source: https://huggingface.co/Nanbeige/Nanbeige4.2-3B
  Source: https://huggingface.co/upstage/Solar-Open2-250B
  Source: https://huggingface.co/shafire/Zero-Gemma4-E4B-OpenZero-GGUF
  Post: https://x.com/LocalAiCherry/status/2082461699622228141

The July 22 story was medals and first proofs; the delta is a checkable ten-result bundle at Sol-API-priced token cost.
  Source: https://openai.com/index/ten-advances-in-mathematics/
  Repo: https://github.com/openai/ten-proofs

- **Karpathy replaces pelican-SVG with a $10 Middle-earth world** - [[Andrej Karpathy]] gave [[Anthropic|Claude Opus 5]] the first paragraph of *The Lord of the Rings*, a 1M-token budget, and asked for a Three.js render. Two hours and about 5,500 lines later he had a playable world—and a clean demo that agents still struggle to natively watch what they build.
  Source: https://karpathy.ai/lotr-movie/
  Post: https://x.com/karpathy/status/2083749667410727319

- **Pokee-Isaac claims real 10M-token agent context on one GPU** - [[Pokee-Isaac]] is pitched as a 28B proprietary agentic model with a native 10M-token window, author-reported 93.3% RULER at 10M, and single-GPU deploy from an RTX 4090-class card. Treat the "world's first" framing as vendor claim; the room question is whether long-horizon agents finally stop chunking.
  Source: https://pokee.ai/
  Source: https://docs.pokee.ai/docs/models
  Post: https://x.com/Pokee_AI/status/2084682445648216383

### Security
- **Bitcoin red team runs on Kimi K3 while OpenAI sits out** - Rob Hamilton's responders say LLMs are already being used to steal bitcoin, and they are burning through [[Kimi|Kimi K3]] plus about $20K in credits while [[OpenAI]] publicly chides the effort. [[Prem]] launched prem-router and sponsored credits so researchers can reach open cyber-capable models without shipping prompts to a reluctant lab.
  Post: https://x.com/Rob1Ham/status/2083546478409056301
  Post: https://x.com/Rob1Ham/status/2084523368783438198
  Post: https://x.com/premai_io/status/2084552134444662986
  Source: https://www.premai.io/

- **Shai-Hulud hits keyv and 2B monthly npm installs** - After May's Mini Shai-Hulud wave, attackers compromised the GitHub account behind `keyv` and its cacheable family, then wormed credential stealers across hundreds of packages totaling over 2 billion monthly installs. Poisoned releases shipped with valid GitHub Actions provenance, so "signed" no longer means "safe."
  Source: https://www.aikido.dev/blog/keyv-and-friends-compromised-in-npm-supply-chain-attack
  Source: https://devops.com/fast-moving-shai-hulud-attack-infects-npm-packages-with-2-billion-monthly-downloads/

### Big Tech Moves
- **OpenAI cuts GPT-5.6 Luna 80% and Terra 20%** - [[OpenAI]] passed runtime-efficiency gains into API pricing: [[GPT-5.6|Luna]] drops to $0.20/$1.20 per million tokens, Terra to $2/$12, while Sol stays put and gets a premium Fast mode. High-volume agent work just got cheaper on OpenAI's stack.
  Source: https://openai.com/index/advancing-the-price-performance-frontier-with-gpt-5-6/
  Post: https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2082878156483219672
