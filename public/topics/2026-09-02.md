### Security
- **Abliterlitics measures what abliteration actually changed** - Nathan Sapwell's [[Abliterlitics]] takes one base model and measures what Heretic, Huihui, AEON, Abliterix, and friends actually changed in weights, KL, capability benches, and HarmBench. On [[Gemma]] 4 E4B he reports Heretic as the best capability/refusal tradeoff at 95.5% ASR with KL=0.002, and the most-downloaded variant as the most damaged; treat those as author-reported [[Security]] forensics, not a how-to.
