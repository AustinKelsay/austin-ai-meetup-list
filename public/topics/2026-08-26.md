### Models & Research
- **Closed model releases** - [[OpenAI]] refreshed GPT-5.6 Sol/Luna for ChatGPT and gated GPT-5.6-Cyber behind Daybreak; [[xAI]] shipped Imagine Image 2.0 and [[Grok]] 4.6; [[Google]] cut [[Gemini]] 3.7 Flash's introductory token price to half 3.6 Flash's original rate; and [[GLM]]-5.3 launched in the API with weights still promised, not shipped. The closed wave is splitting into default chat, long-running agents, creative tools, and access-controlled specialists.

The useful comparison is deployment shape and license, not one blended leaderboard.

- **Open-model hype and adoption barely overlap** - Hugging Face found one shared repository between its 2026 top-25 download and like lists; models under 1B parameters take 83% of declared-size downloads, while [[Qwen]] has grown to 151,448 derivatives. The useful question is which [[Open Models|open artifacts]] became infrastructure instead of launch-week theater.
  Source: https://huggingface.co/blog/state-of-open-models-summer-2026

### Security
- **OpenAI pauses frontier training after Astra approaches critical cyber** - [[OpenAI]] says preliminary evals put [[Astra]] near its Critical cybersecurity threshold, prompting a two-week pause in deployment-bound RL and leaving its largest planned frontier run on hold. This is the August 5 math model returning as a training-security problem.
  Source: https://openai.com/index/responding-next-frontier-critical-cyber-capabilities/
  Source: https://openai.com/index/pacing-model-development-cyber-capabilities/

- **CoSnitch turns Copilot memory into persistent attacker state** - Varonis chained automatic prompt execution, connected-app exfiltration, and memory poisoning in [[Microsoft]] Copilot Personal. Microsoft patched CVE-2026-24301 on August 18; Varonis says it saw no exploitation in the wild, but password and session rotation would not clear the poisoned memory.
  Source: https://www.varonis.com/blog/cosnitch
  Source: https://msrc.microsoft.com/update-guide/vulnerability/CVE-2026-24301

- **Google ships a runnable zero-trust agent failure lab** - [[Google]]'s open ADK demo assumes the model will be tricked, then moves hard guarantees outside the prompt with signed writes, gVisor isolation, and deterministic gateways. This is a concrete builder answer to the agent incidents on the last two boards.
  Blog: https://developers.googleblog.com/build-zero-trust-ai-agents-with-googles-agent-development-kit/
  Repo: https://github.com/GoogleCloudPlatform/generative-ai/tree/main/agents/adk/zero-trust-agents

### Big Tech Moves
- **Jalapeño posts its first silicon results** - [[OpenAI]] says its first custom inference chip delivered 1.5–1.9× more work per watt and 1.7–3.6× lower end-to-end latency across three public models than the comparison systems it tested. These are vendor-run results, but the June chip announcement now has working silicon and a planned year-end deployment.
  Source: https://openai.com/index/jalapeno-first-results/
  Source: https://openai.com/index/the-full-stack-behind-abundant-intelligence/

- **Claude will watermark text globally for the EU AI Act** - [[Anthropic]] says future Claude models will nudge token choices to mark generated text without hidden characters, extra tokens, or user identifiers, and will attach C2PA credentials to supported files. A regional transparency rule is becoming a global model behavior.
  Source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-text-watermark
  Source: https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/strong-backing-code-practice-transparency-ai-generated-content
