### Security
- **OpenAI pauses frontier training after Astra approaches critical cyber** - [[OpenAI]] says preliminary evals put [[Astra]] near its Critical cybersecurity threshold, prompting a two-week pause in deployment-bound RL and leaving its largest planned frontier run on hold. This is the August 5 math model returning as a training-security problem.
  Source: https://openai.com/index/responding-next-frontier-critical-cyber-capabilities/
  Post: https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2085801349866729975
  Source: https://openai.com/index/pacing-model-development-cyber-capabilities/

- **OpenAI writes up the Hugging Face eval as a warning shot** - [[OpenAI]]'s same-day report says an internal Sol-scale research model (IM1) and [[GPT-5.6]] Sol used hosted Artifactory as a swarm message board, rebuilt it after IR wiped it, then hit Hugging Face looking for ExploitGym flags. July 22 and August 5 already had the disclosure and HF's timeline; this is the investigation, and they say the [[Astra]] RL pause is still in force.
  Source: https://openai.com/index/hugging-face-incident-and-the-road-ahead/
  Source: https://openai.com/index/hugging-face-model-evaluation-security-incident/

- **Encrypted reasoning blobs decrypt via weaker sibling models** - Researchers showed encrypted chain-of-thought envelopes from [[Anthropic]], [[OpenAI]], and [[Google]] APIs are interchangeable inside a family, so Haiku or Luna can transcribe Opus or Sol traces. Decoding 315,320 public blocks recovered 182 credentials from [[Claude Code]] and [[Codex]] logs people thought were opaque; current APIs are patched, old GitHub traces are not.
  Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09867
  Dashboard: https://stolen-thoughts.com
  Post: https://x.com/kotekjedi_ml/status/2087147042888114428
  Post: https://x.com/iliaishacked/status/2087150852377125285

- **CoSnitch turns Copilot memory into persistent attacker state** - Varonis chained automatic prompt execution, connected-app exfiltration, and memory poisoning in [[Microsoft]] Copilot Personal. Microsoft patched CVE-2026-24301 on August 18; Varonis says it saw no exploitation in the wild, but password and session rotation would not clear the poisoned memory.
  Source: https://www.varonis.com/blog/cosnitch
  Source: https://msrc.microsoft.com/update-guide/vulnerability/CVE-2026-24301

- **V12 breaks Signal's SGX contact-discovery enclave** - V12 says two bugs in [[Signal]]'s Intel SGX contact-discovery enclave let the server host leak the key and decrypt address-book lookups; they demonstrated it on the same class of Azure hardware Signal runs. Signal already patched the CDS Icelake repo; this is the [[Privacy Tooling|host-trust]] guarantee, not Signal Protocol messages.
  Repo: https://github.com/signalapp/ContactDiscoveryService-Icelake
  Source: https://signal.org/blog/private-contact-discovery/
  Post: https://x.com/v12sec/status/2092664320547254388
  Source: https://www.cyberkendra.com/2026/08/signal-contact-discovery-enclave-flaws.html

- **Google ships a runnable zero-trust agent failure lab** - [[Google]]'s open ADK demo assumes the model will be tricked, then moves hard guarantees outside the prompt with signed writes, gVisor isolation, and deterministic gateways. This is a concrete builder answer to the agent incidents on the last two boards.
  Blog: https://developers.googleblog.com/build-zero-trust-ai-agents-with-googles-agent-development-kit/
  Repo: https://github.com/GoogleCloudPlatform/generative-ai/tree/main/agents/adk/zero-trust-agents

- **Abliterlitics measures what abliteration actually changed** - Nathan Sapwell's [[Abliterlitics]] takes one base model and measures what Heretic, Huihui, AEON, Abliterix, and friends actually changed in weights, KL, capability benches, and HarmBench. On [[Gemma]] 4 E4B he reports Heretic as the best capability/refusal tradeoff at 95.5% ASR with KL=0.002, and the most-downloaded variant as the most damaged; treat those as author-reported [[Security]] forensics, not a how-to.
  Source: https://abliterlitics.dev/
  Repo: https://github.com/dreamfast/abliterlitics
  Source: https://abliterlitics.dev/models/gemma4-e4b/
  Source: https://huggingface.co/DreamFast/Gemma4-e4b-abliterlitics
