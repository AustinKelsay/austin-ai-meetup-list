# GPT-5.6

GPT-5.6 is the OpenAI model-release line tracked in the June 24 rumor/cadence topic and the July 8 proprietary-release topic, where the wiki records the shift from leak chatter to official preview and launch framing. OpenAI later reported that retained reasoning and compaction in the [[Codex]] harness raised GPT-5.6 Sol from 13.3% to 38.3% on ARC-AGI-3; treat that as a vendor-reported harness effect, not a new model.
