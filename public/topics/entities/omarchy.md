# Omarchy

Omarchy is DHH's opinionated Arch Linux distribution built on Hyprland and Quickshell. The August 14, 2026 Quattro (4.0.0) release treats coding-agent CLIs as first-class, lazy-loaded OS defaults rather than a pinned vendor agent, and the following two weeks brought DHH's petabyte-of-ISOs growth claim plus the Omacom Foundation's $10M nonprofit backing.
